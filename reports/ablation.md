Query counts: exact_id 43, keyword 35, multi_hop 24, natural 84, supersession 28.

Blended recall averages this away. `exact_id` is where BM25 earns its place and dense retrieval fails; `supersession` and `multi_hop` are the designed slices, and are the hardest by construction.
