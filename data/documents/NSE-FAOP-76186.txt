Annexure – 3
Important guidelines in case of switchover of trading system to Disaster Recovery (DR) Site
1. Kindly note below points after switch over to DR site:
a. Trades of primary site will be available in Previous Trades window.
b. Trades can be modified/cancelled using Multiple Trade Modification and Cancellation window
respectively.
c. Messages of primary site will be available in TWS Message Area.
2. If user renames or deletes the User folder and re-login the NEAT terminal, then data of primary site
shall not be available in the functional window as mentioned above point.
