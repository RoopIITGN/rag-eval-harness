All Members,
Introduction of Futures and Options Contracts on Nifty India FPI 150 Index
Exchange is pleased to inform members that with reference to approval received from SEBI, Futures and
Options contracts on Nifty India FPI 150 Index shall be made available for trading in Future & Options segment
w.e.f. August 12, 2026.
The relevant information about the monthly futures and options contracts are as given in Annexure 1.
The settlement procedure and details shall be intimated separately by the respective clearing corporations.
Members may note that there is no change in the structure of any of the reports.
The contract.gz file and MII contract (NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz) and spread
(NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz) files made available to trading members on the NSE Extranet (Path:
/faoftp/faocommon) from August 11, 2026, end of day will reflect the new monthly futures and options
contracts. Further, members are advised to load the above file in the trading application before trading on
August 12, 2026.
